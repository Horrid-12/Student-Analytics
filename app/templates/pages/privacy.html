Privacy Policy
How we handle your data and privacy.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the GitHub Student Analytics Platform ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our service.

2. Information We Collect

When you use our platform, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Your name, university email address, and role.
  • OAuth Data: If you link your GitHub or LinkedIn accounts, we collect public profile information (such as usernames and avatars) provided by those services.
  • Academic Data: Student roll numbers (PRN), division, batch, and other enrollment details required for verification.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To verify student identity and academic status.
  • To aggregate and display GitHub repository analytics and leaderboards.
  • To provide administrative features for faculty and staff.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information in the following circumstances:

  • Within the Platform: Certain data (like GitHub statistics) is visible to faculty and other verified students on the leaderboards.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, subpoena, or other legal processes.

5. Third-Party Services

Our platform integrates with third-party APIs (GitHub, LinkedIn, Google). Your interactions with these services are governed by their respective privacy policies.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact your university's academic administration or the platform administrator.
