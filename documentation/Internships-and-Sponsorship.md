## Future of the repo — open for future use

The intended future of this repository is **to be absorbed by Rootkit Consultancy**: it stays here as the complete record of the platform built for MIT-WPU and as a codebase Rootkit can use and extend in its own development work. It is **open for future use**, not an open call for outside contributors.

- The code, issue history and documentation in this repo are the single source of truth for what was built at MIT-WPU.
- Work in progress is merged into `main` so the repository is coherent when it is handed over; long-running branches are integrated back rather than diverging permanently.
- The docs — [`README.md`](../README.md), [`Features List.md`](./Features%20List.md), [`Bug Tracker.md`](./Bug%20Tracker.md) and [`Taskflow.md`](./Taskflow.md) — stay in step with what shipped so the next team can pick the project up from here.
- Rootkit Consultancy's internship offer (recorded above) grows directly out of this work; the repository is the technical record that supports that handover.

## Sponsorship

**Status: open for future use — no commercial sponsor formally recorded yet.**

- **Supporting institution:** Dr. Vishwanath Karad MIT World Peace University, Pune — the university the Student and Teacher Portal was built for, and where the team's work on it began.

Sponsorship is welcome from individuals or organisations that want to support the platform and the students maintaining it.

Useful forms of support:

- Hosting, database and cache credits for the production deployment (Vercel, Neon Postgres, Upstash Redis).
- CI/CD minutes, tooling licences, or test infrastructure that lets the team run the full suite on every PR.
- Mentorship and code reviews from engineers who have shipped production Python web apps.

Sponsors should open an issue or contact the maintainers through the repository to agree on scope and visibility before any support is announced here. Once confirmed, record the sponsor, what they provide and the dates in this section.
